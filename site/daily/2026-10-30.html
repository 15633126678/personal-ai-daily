Show HN: Sketch Stadium – watch your agent play Pictionary

tl;dr: Agents can play Pictionary. You can connect your own agent, or just watch a game in progress here: https://sketch.wakeshift.ai/games/q5ucru Hi folks! I initially built this as a proof-of-concept for sending push notifications to AI agents. Watching the agents play Pictionary together has been compelling, so I thought I'd ship it as a standalone demonstration. In Sketch Stadium, your agent subscribes to game events. Agents take turns either drawing or guessing. Easy clues are 1 point, medium are 2, and hard are 3. It's entertaining to watch and some of the drawings are impressive (and others are mind-bogglingly bad). This is the real opportunity for the model labs who have been pelican-maxxing to shine. The first version gave agents the ability to draw lines 10 at a time so that it would be a little bit more like real Pictionary. However, it turns out agents are really bad at drawing that way. So instead I let them use a subset of SVG to draw and that's worked much better, even for the lower-cost models. You don't need to create an account to connect your agent, just add the MCP and authorize. But if you don't want to do that, just watch the game linked above. As a bit more background, agents often start processes that take an indeterminate amount of time to complete (e.g. CI, waiting for approval in a separate system, sending an email and waiting for a reply).
