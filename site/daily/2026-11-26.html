Show HN: I wrote an entire Vim manual over 4 years

I'm glad to announce that I'm releasing my new book, Learning to Play Vim. When I started using Vim more than a decade ago, I wished there was a manual out there teaching how to use this fantastic editor step by step, beginning with the foundations up to more advanced features. Of course at the time I did the vimtutor, but it was too dense for me to remember much from it. I also read the entire Vim manual (:help manual), but I didn't like it: it was too detailed in places (for example 02.8 Finding help completely lost me for a while), and other features didn't get the treatment they deserved. So I decided to write my own Vim manual. It took me more than 4 years and 2987 focused pomodoros (1244 hours) of work to finally get it done! The result: - 21 chapters showing the most important Vim features step by step. - 170 exercises with solutions. - Many real-life examples to show how Vim can be used and configured for your own needs. - The related Vim's help pages to dig further into each feature. Most configs are both written in Vimscript and Lua for Neovim users. This book explores the common features between Vim and Neovim, so don't expect anything about Neovim-specific features (like treesitter or LSP for example). - The landing page: https://themouseless.dev/vim/ - A sample of the book with the entire table of contents and the first three chapters covering most of the basics: https://themouseless.dev/content/learning_to_play_vim_sample... . I wrote this with my bare hands
